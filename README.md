# Restaurant-Segmentation-Analysis


 Restaurants face significant challenges in managing inventory effectively due to the inherent complexities of their operations. Unpredictable customer demand, influenced by factors such as seasonality, special events, and daily variability, makes accurate forecasting difficult. Perishable ingredients often go unused before their expiration, leading to financial losses and contributing to food waste, a pressing environmental concern. Manual inventory tracking methods are time-consuming, prone to errors, and can result in either overstocking, which ties up capital and increases waste, or understocking, which leads to menu shortages and dissatisfied customers. Furthermore, a lack of insights into supplier performance can cause issues such as delayed or suboptimal deliveries, disrupting kitchen workflows. The absence of integration between existing systems, such as Point-of-Sale (POS) platforms and supplier tools, further limits the ability to make data-driven decisions. These challenges underscore the need for a comprehensive and intelligent inventory management solution tailored to the unique demands of the restaurant industry.
